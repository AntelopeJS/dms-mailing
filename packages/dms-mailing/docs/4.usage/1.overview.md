# Overview

The Overview is the module's landing page. It answers "are our e-mails reaching people?" for the selected period (7, 30 or 90 days), compared with the previous period by default. Test sends are left out of every figure.

![The Mailing overview with reach, engagement and list health KPIs and the send volume chart](../images/overview.png)

## Figures

| Group | KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Sends | E-mails sent in the period |
| Reach | Deliverability | Share of sends the provider delivered |
| Engagement | Open rate | Share of delivered e-mails opened |
| Engagement | Click rate | Share of delivered e-mails clicked |
| List health | Bounces | Lower is better |
| List health | Unsubscribes | Lower is better |

The **Send volume** chart plots the sends per day against the compared period, day 1 on day 1.

## Below the fold

![Funnel, best opened templates and open rate by domain](../images/overview-lower.png)

- **Needs attention** lists what to act on (sends with a problem, templates missing a workspace locale), each with a verb such as **Review problems**, which opens the Sends page on its Problems tab.
- **Funnel · sent to clicked** shows each step with its count and rate, for all templates, transactional ones or the `marketing` category, and names the biggest drop.
- **Best opened templates** and **Open rate by domain** (domains with 5 or more deliveries) cover the last 30 days.

## Toolbar

**New template** sits in the page header. Under it, the toolbar shows whether the provider is connected, when the figures were last updated, **Direct** / **Pause**, the period, the comparison menu and the **7D** / **30D** / **90D** switch.

The comparison menu ("vs previous period" by default) compares the figures with the period just before, with the same dates a year earlier ("vs previous year"), or with nothing ("No comparison"): the KPI changes and the dashed series of the volume chart then disappear.

In **Direct** the figures refresh as e-mails are sent and the provider reports back, at most every 5 seconds. **Pause** freezes them and counts the new sends until you resume (the L key switches too). The choice is shared with the Sends page.

Until the workspace has a provider and a template, the page shows a setup checklist instead of the figures; **Resume the setup** reopens it later.
